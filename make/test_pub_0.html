• Size Stabilizers in Two-electrode Synthesis of ZnO Nanorods

    R V Korol, O M Yanchuk, O V Marchuk, V F Orlov, I A Moroz and O A Vyshnevskyi,    Phys. & Chem. of Solid State   2021   22 (2), 380-387,   10.15330/pcss.22.2.380-387.
    ×

    We modify and optimize a synthesis of ZnO nanoparticles by electrodeposition by adding auxiliary stabilizers to reduce the size and narrow its distribution in the target product.
